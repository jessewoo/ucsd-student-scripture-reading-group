About us

We're a group of UCSD students who found God during college — and it changed everything. What started as our own honest questions turned into a love for reading Scripture together.

Now we just want to share that with others. No pressure, no perfect answers — just students inviting more students to open the Bible and talk about it, together.

We're an official student ministry connected to WAV Church — come once, come every week, or never come back. No commitment, no sign-up required to just show up.
